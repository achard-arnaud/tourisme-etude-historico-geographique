# Sri Lanka — longue durée jusqu’à 1948

Worked corpus for `tourisme-etude-historico-geographique`.

## Scope of run 2
This pass does not rewrite every ancient arc. It reopens the **Portuguese → VOC → British bridge** after a Jaffna field visit on 18 August 2026 and a systematic crawl of the discoverable Stichting Nederland–Sri Lanka corpus.

The new material is routed into five questions:
1. Was the VOC an ally of Kandy or a replacement coastal sovereign?
2. What did a chain of forts actually govern beyond military architecture?
3. How did cinnamon, elephants, pearls, textiles, corvée and slavery form one political economy?
4. Why is Jaffna/Mannar an India-facing system rather than a northern appendix of the island?
5. Which Dutch administrative technologies survived into British Ceylon?

Read `09_output/report.md` for the narrative delta and `05_sources/stichting_nederland_sri_lanka_inventory.md` for the institutional corpus inventory.

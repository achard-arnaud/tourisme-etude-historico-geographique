# A07b_coffee_collapse_and_tea_conversion — Coffee collapse and tea conversion

## Entry rupture
Coffee rust and the solvency crisis of the 1870s break the plantation model built around Arabica coffee.

## Causal question
Why did a destroyed crop become a different crop rather than no crop?

## TL;DR
Shell only. Three rival mechanisms are preregistered before claim promotion: biological constraint, capital-form transition, and labour-system conservation. The discriminating object is the lag between rust arrival, ownership transfer, resident labour density, and crop conversion.

## Drivers / amplifiers / consequences / non-causes
- Candidate driver: biological constraint from coffee rust — unresolved.
- Candidate driver: ownership and financing transition toward agency-house / joint-stock capital — unresolved, priority hypothesis.
- Candidate driver: preservation of a resident labour system through year-round tea work — unresolved.
- Candidate amplifier: sunk coffee-era roads and railway inherited by successor crops.
- False lead candidate: a smooth coffee → tea sequence that omits cinchona.
- Non-cause discipline: do not let the rust narrative absorb capital, labour, infrastructure or market mechanisms before the lag test is run.

## What changes the optimum?
Evidence showing whether conversion timing follows the rust front, ownership transfer, or resident labour density. Smallholder coffee is a gate because it tests biological constraint without corporate-capital access.

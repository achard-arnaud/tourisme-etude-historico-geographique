# A02 — Ancient Indian Ocean exchange

- Entry rupture: the island becomes archaeologically visible as a node in overlapping South Asian and western Indian Ocean exchange systems.
- Causal question: what did Sri Lanka produce, transform, redistribute and mediate, and at which scales?
- Zooms: Z0 artefact → Z1 site/port → Z3 Indian Ocean network → Z4 interregional system → return local.
- Primary HILs: economy/infrastructure; geography/environment; regional-global system.
- Exit rupture: exchange evidence must remain separated from later ethnic/national narratives and from direct-contact inflation.
- Related investigations: glass working, Mediterranean/Taprobane exchange, Nāga traditions and reception.

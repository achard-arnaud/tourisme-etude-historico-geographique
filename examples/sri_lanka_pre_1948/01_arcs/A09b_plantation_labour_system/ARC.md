# A09b — Plantation labour system

evidence_status: partial
- corpus: pre-1948
- span: 1830–1948
- threads: labour, land, water, caste/ethnicity, trade

## Causal question
How was a resident plantation workforce constructed, and what made the labour system survive the crop that first assembled it?

## Separations
Imported labour recruitment/debt, Kandyan land alienation, line-room housing and later citizenship status are distinct mechanisms. Gendered plucking is treated as labour organization rather than timeless culture.

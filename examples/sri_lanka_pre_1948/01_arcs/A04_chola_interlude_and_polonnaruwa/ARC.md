# A04 — Chola interlude and Polonnaruwa

evidence_status: partial

## Entry rupture

Chola conquest and administration shift the political centre toward Polonnaruwa and embed the island in a South Indian imperial and military system.

## Causal question

How did conquest, post-Chola reconstruction, military corporations, fiscal capacity and new kingship strategies reshape Polonnaruwa without reducing the period to a simple Sinhala/Tamil or Buddhist/Hindu binary?

## Evidence slice in Run 14

This run industrialises only four pre-existing boxes: the Velaikkara inscription, Galpota as royal legitimation, the Magha historiographic guardrail, and Vijayabahu's reconquest in a regional window. It is a measured vertical slice, not full-arc closure.

## Run39 — monetary stress before the rupture

The coin sequence adds a useful material indicator to the late-Polonnaruwa problem. The earlier gold Kahavanu/Massa tradition gives way increasingly to base-metal and copper issues; silver may survive as a wash rather than as the substance of the coin, and a Dharmasokadeva issue of 1208 is copper. This is a documented monetary change, not merely a literary story about decline.

The causal reading remains deliberately bounded. Liyanagamage's specialist study treats the drain from Parakramabahu I's military commitments, foreign garrisons and costly royal programmes as a plausible explanation for the disappearance of precious-metal coinage, but also notes that debasement was already visible under Vijayabahu I and that a larger volume of trade could itself increase demand for lower-value coin. Run39 therefore treats monetary debasement as a **stress signal and possible amplifier**, not as proof of a modern-style inflation crisis or as the single reason Polonnaruwa fell.

The stronger synthesis is cumulative: a court with more constrained fiscal and monetary resources would have had less room to absorb succession struggles, maintain military and patronage networks, and respond to external attack. This mechanism is plausible but remains lower-confidence than the underlying numismatic fact.

## Exit rupture / bridge forward

Magha's seizure in 1215 and the subsequent fragmentation form the exit boundary; the causes of dry-zone contraction and mobile-capital politics remain for later research. Run39 adds monetary/fiscal resilience to that transition without replacing the existing political, military and environmental explanations.

## Partial guardrail

The promoted records have their own lineage, but the arc itself remains `partial` and must not be interpreted as fully researched or recap-ready. In particular, no purchasing-power series, price evidence or quantified causal weight exists that would allow monetary debasement to be ranked mechanically above succession instability, military expenditure, trade change, regional fragmentation or invasion.

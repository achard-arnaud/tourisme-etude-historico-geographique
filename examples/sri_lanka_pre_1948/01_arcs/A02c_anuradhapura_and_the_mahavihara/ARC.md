## Causal question
How much of the Mihintale foundation narrative can be separated into modern commemoration, transmitted chronicle tradition, and independently attested monastic institutions?

## Evidence rule
The 2024 stele is T0 for the 2024 act of commemoration only. No claim about the third-century-BCE sequence may exceed confidence C unless independently anchored in contemporaneous material evidence. Mahinda IV's tenth-century slabs establish later institutional administration, not the antiquity of the conversion narrative.

# Jaffna caste codification under colonial rule

Colonial administrations did not invent caste in Jaffna, but registration, law, taxation and labour needs could stabilize selected social categories and increase the administrative leverage of particular intermediaries.

## Sources
CAMBRIDGE-CLASSIFICATION; CAMBRIDGE-JAFFNA-SLAVERY.

## Limits
Avoid a single colonial master-cause: hierarchy, local agency, slavery/debt/service and administrative classification interacted differently across time.

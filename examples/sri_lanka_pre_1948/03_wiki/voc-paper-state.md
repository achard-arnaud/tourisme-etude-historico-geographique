# VOC paper state in coastal Ceylon

VOC government combined forts, courts, taxation, service obligations and registers. Thombos and legal classification made land, persons and obligations more legible to colonial administration and created path dependence later available to British rule.

## Sources
CAMBRIDGE-CLASSIFICATION; CAMBRIDGE-LAWMAKING.

## Limits
Administrative legibility did not mean complete control, and local actors also used records and courts strategically.

# Run18 — coffee→tea hypotheses

1. **H1 biological determinism (U):** does conversion timing track the rust front estate by estate?
2. **H2 capital-form determinism (U, priority):** does conversion timing track ownership transfer, agency-house consolidation and factory-capital investment, lagging rust?
3. **H3 labour-system conservation (U):** do estates with denser resident labour forces convert faster regardless of rust timing or ownership?

## Discriminating test
Build a lag table from colonial administration reports with district/estate-year acreage by crop, rust incidence, ownership form/transfer and resident estate population. Do not promote a dominant cause before this table exists.

## Open subquestions
- How long and where did cinchona intervene between coffee and tea?
- Did cinchona price collapse precede tea conversion locally?
- Which agency houses financed first tea factories and under what corporate form?
- Did year-round tea work materially change labour retention relative to coffee's harvest seasonality?

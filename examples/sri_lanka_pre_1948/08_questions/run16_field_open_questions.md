# Run 16 — open questions (Mihintale / Abhayagiri)

1. Which early Brahmi cave inscriptions at Mihintale independently attest monastic occupation, and what are their secure dates?
2. Can any contemporary inscription support a third-century BCE institutional claim attributed to Mahinda, independently of the chronicles?
3. How should BE 236 and BE 284 be mapped against short/long chronologies without silently choosing one?
4. Is the seven-person mission roster stable across Mahavamsa/Dipavamsa recensions, or a later harmonisation?
5. What is the earliest attestation of the Ambasthala mango-riddle episode and its toponymic link?
6. What evidence distinguishes Mahavihara delimitation as historical institution-building from retrospective legitimation?
7. What is securely known archaeologically about the earliest phase of Mihindu Seya?
8. Can the 2024 restoration's 'enemy destruction' framing be tied to a specific destruction horizon, or should it remain commissioning rhetoric?
9. What do the Mahinda IV tablets reveal about staff, revenues, discipline and land tenure, and which elements can be compared to Abhayagiri administration?
10. When will the 17 Abhayagiri bronzes receive laboratory/conservation publication beyond stylistic comparison? Review after 2026-09-19.
11. Do the bronzes carry iconographic features diagnostic of Mahayana/Vajrayana affiliation, or is that inference premature?
12. Does the August 2026 announcement alter heritage presentation of Abhayagiri relative to Mahavihara, and if so through what documented institutional mechanism?
13. Are multiple AFP republications being mistakenly counted as corroboration anywhere else in the corpus?
14. Can excavation records locate the bronzes stratigraphically enough to distinguish deposition date from manufacture date?
15. What material evidence supports or contradicts the chronicle's institutional rivalry between Abhayagiri and Mahavihara?

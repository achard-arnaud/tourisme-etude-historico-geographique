# Stichting Nederland–Sri Lanka — crawl inventory

## Crawl status
The origin site intermittently returned gateway errors during this run. The inventory therefore uses a **systematic search-index crawl of the discoverable site surface**, plus pages returned directly by the search index. It is intended as a research inventory, **not a bit-for-bit mirror** and not a claim that every WordPress attachment or unindexed page was recovered.

## Why it is a first-line source family
The Foundation was established in The Hague in 1976 and works on Dutch–Sri Lankan cultural and academic relations. Its board/past board includes Sri Lanka specialists and academics. Most importantly for this run, the **Dutch Forts in Sri Lanka** exhibition was produced with the Sri Lanka Department of Archaeology, Galle Heritage Foundation, Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Colombo and the Foundation Documentation Monuments VOC, and was authored/compiled by Lodewijk Wagenaar.

Classification: **T2 institutional synthesis**. Selected items can be marked **specialist institutional anchor**. That status reflects expertise and provenance, not promotion to T1 academic research.

## Fort subcorpus
The exhibition announces six general banners — Dutch forts; alliance against Portugal 1638–1658; occupied coastal area; construction/maintenance; administration/exploitation; batteries/sentry boxes — and dedicated banners for **Galle, Matara, Jaffna, Mannar, Trincomalee, Batticaloa and Colombo**.

This subcorpus is particularly valuable because it converts fort architecture into a distributed map of colonial logistics. It is used as an anchor map, then bridged to academic work on law, fiscal registration, labour and local intermediaries.

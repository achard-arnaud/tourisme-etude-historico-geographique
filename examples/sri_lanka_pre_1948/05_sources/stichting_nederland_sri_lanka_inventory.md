# Stichting Nederland–Sri Lanka — systematic discoverable-site inventory

## Crawl status
The origin site intermittently returned **502/timeout errors** during this run. The inventory therefore combines search-index discovery, cached/indexed page text and the pages that could be resolved individually. It covers the **discoverable indexed site surface relevant to the Foundation and its historical/heritage work**, but is deliberately **not described as a bit-for-bit mirror**: unindexed WordPress attachments or inaccessible media may be absent.

## Why it is a first-line source family
The Foundation was established in The Hague in 1976 and works on Dutch–Sri Lankan cultural and academic relations. Current and past boards include historians, conflict researchers and heritage specialists. The key **Dutch Forts in Sri Lanka** exhibition was produced with the Sri Lanka Department of Archaeology, Galle Heritage Foundation, Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Colombo and Foundation Documentation Monuments VOC, and authored/compiled by Lodewijk Wagenaar.

Classification: **T2 institutional synthesis**. Selected items are **specialist institutional anchors** because of narrow expertise and documented provenance. They do **not** become T1 academic research.

## Fort subcorpus
The exhibition announces six general banners — **Dutch forts; alliance against Portugal 1638–1658; occupied coastal area; construction/maintenance; administration/exploitation; batteries/sentry boxes** — and dedicated banners for **Galle, Matara, Jaffna, Mannar, Trincomalee, Batticaloa and Colombo**.

Its analytical value is not that every sentence is authoritative. It converts architecture into a **distributed logistics map**. The OS therefore uses it to locate claims and bridges, then follows load-bearing statements into archives/T1 scholarship on law, fiscal registration, labour, trade and intermediaries.

## Crawl conclusion
No additional indexed page discovered in this run justified a new main causal branch. The remaining structural/news pages are retained for provenance and navigation rather than injected into the fresco. This is an explicit anti-pollution decision, not an assumption that they are uninteresting.

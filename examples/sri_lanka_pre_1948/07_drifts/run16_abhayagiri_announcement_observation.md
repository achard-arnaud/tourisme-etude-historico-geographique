# Run16 observation — Abhayagiri discovery announcement as a heritage-governance event

The 17-bronze discovery is both an archaeological event and a public heritage announcement.

## What is observable
- announcement date: 19 August 2026;
- announcer quoted by AFP: Thusitha Herath, Central Cultural Fund excavation project lead;
- institutional frame: excavation inside the UNESCO-listed Sacred City of Anuradhapura;
- provisional dating language: stylistic comparison, with further analysis explicitly pending;
- expected public display: stated as an intention within about one month.

## What is not asserted
Run16 does **not** infer a political, religious or publicity motive from the timing, institutional speaker or heritage frame. Syndicated AFP copies are treated as one source family, not corroboration.

## Drift trap
A fresh, visually striking discovery at Abhayagiri must not be used retroactively to close the older Mahavihara/Abhayagiri doctrinal rivalry or to assign the bronzes to Mahayana/Vajrayana before technical/iconographic publication.

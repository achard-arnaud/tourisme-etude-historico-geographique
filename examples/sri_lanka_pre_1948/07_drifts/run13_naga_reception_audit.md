# Run 13 drift audit — Nāga category and modern reception

## Drift under review
Treating “the Nāgas” as a settled ancient ethnicity and then assigning that population retrospectively to a modern Sinhala or Tamil national genealogy.

## Ancient layer
The chronicle material is stored as `tradition`; modern reconstruction is stored as `inference` or open `question`. Personal names, literary categories and toponyms cannot by themselves establish a stable ethnic population equivalent to modern national categories.

## Reception layer
Modern Sinhala and Tamil nationalist historiographies both make claims on deep antiquity. The relevant modern sources in Run 13 (`VENUGOPAL-NATIONALISM-2018`, `ICG-SINHALA-NATIONALISM-2007`) are evidence for political/historiographic reception, not anchors for ancient Nāga identity.

## Guardrails
- No Run 13 Nāga claim above confidence B.
- Chronicle episode ≠ contemporaneous population census.
- Toponymic resemblance ≠ `source_fact`.
- Modern nationalist use belongs here in the drift ledger or the post-1948 reception layer, not silently inside ancient claims.

## Open
The epigraphic inventory remains unresolved and is explicitly retained in `08_questions/run13_open_questions.md`.

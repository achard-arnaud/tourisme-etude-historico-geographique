# Run 16 — militarisation source-position audit

This intake is routed through historiography/drift review before HIL analysis.

## Source-family positions

- **Tamil-nationalist/local outlets**: strong local event visibility; framing of the north/east as occupied Tamil homeland is an editorial position. Use as T4 leads and name the family in `bounded_by` where independent confirmation is absent.
- **Sinhala-nationalist outlets**: arguments that troop reduction weakens sovereignty or increases Indian leverage are positions, not neutral causal findings. Preserve named holders and do not universalise.
- **International rights bodies**: OHCHR treaty bodies and HRW document land/commercial activity with institutional or advocacy mandates. Their independence from Sri Lankan official self-reporting is useful but their normative role is recorded.
- **Sri Lankan official records**: Parliament, ministries and military records are primary for policy statements, land-release procedures and institutional self-description; troop/land totals require definition and cross-checking.

## Required drift controls

1. Do not equate military civilian engagement in Mihintale with military land/commercial activity in the north merely because the institution is the same.
2. Do not turn local checkpoint reporting into national trend evidence without a second source family.
3. Do not convert `land released` into `demilitarisation completed`; retained land, formally acquired land and commercial activity are separate denominators.
4. Do not merge post-2009 war-era checkpoints with post-2019 religious-site bag checks/scanners; they belong to different causal chains.
5. Do not infer motive from the timing of religious construction, heritage activity or land retention without direct evidence.

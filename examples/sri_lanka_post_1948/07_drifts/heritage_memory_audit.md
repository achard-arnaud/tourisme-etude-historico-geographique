# Drift audit — heritage in the modern fresco

- **Heritage determinism:** colonial monuments do not explain post-independence nationalism by themselves.
- **Memory = history:** a protected monument may preserve a later story rather than an authentic event; treat commemoration itself as evidence.
- **UNESCO = preservation success:** designation can raise value and tourism while displacing residents; separate material conservation from social continuity.
- **Restitution = repaired colonial relationship:** return of objects can be important without resolving wider structural inequalities.
- **Postwar restoration = reconciliation:** reconstruction of Jaffna Fort is evidence of heritage policy; whether it reconciles communities requires separate research.
- **Event-speaker laundering:** a prestigious seminar programme is a navigation source, not evidence that every speaker’s thesis is established.

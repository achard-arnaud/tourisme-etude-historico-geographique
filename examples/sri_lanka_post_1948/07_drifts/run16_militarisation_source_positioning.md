## Drift traps
1. **Flat occupation narrative:** 'nothing moves' is falsified by documented partial releases; it remains possible that large unresolved holdings persist.
2. **Flat normalization narrative:** a military project at Mihintale does not make northern commercial/land involvement equivalent in reception or coercive context.
3. **Checkpoint conflation:** war-era or post-war north/east checkpoints must not be merged with bag checks/scanners at southern and western religious sites introduced after the 2019 Easter attacks.
4. **Motive inflation:** military construction or land use at a religious site does not by itself establish a doctrinal or ethnic motive; fiscal, security, administrative and religious mechanisms must remain separable.
5. **False corroboration:** a UK Home Office note that cites Tamil Guardian does not constitute a second independent family for the underlying checkpoint event.

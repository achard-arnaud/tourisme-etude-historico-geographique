# Bahasa Indonesia

Malay-based national language selected by Indonesian nationalism rather than the language of the numerically dominant Javanese group.

## Durable interpretation
It demonstrates an alternative design for linguistic decolonisation: a shared lingua franca can become national while regional languages remain socially active.

## Related claims
C-COMP-ID-001; C-COMP-ID-LANG-002.

## Sources
CAMBRIDGE-INDONESIA-LANGUAGE; BPS-INDONESIA-LANGUAGE-2024.

## Limits
It is not a quasi-experiment proving that language choice caused Indonesian unity, democracy or growth.

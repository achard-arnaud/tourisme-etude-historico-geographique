# Northern Province postwar economy

A post-conflict regional economy in which reconstruction had to restore roads, electricity, water, irrigation, administrative capacity and links to markets after prolonged war and underinvestment.

## Durable interpretation
Infrastructure damage is a mechanism of territorial scarring, but reconstruction inputs are not themselves proof of convergence. The current analytical question is whether restored connectivity generates firms, jobs, investment and cross-regional spillovers.

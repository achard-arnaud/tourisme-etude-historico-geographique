# Tamil Nadu

Large federated state of India used as a bounded comparator for Tamil-language politics, anti-caste mobilisation, reservations, education and conversion of human capital into territorial development.

## Durable interpretation
The useful comparison is an institutional package: social-justice mobilisation + broadened education + welfare/infrastructure + diversification + large-market absorption. It is not evidence that reservations or English alone cause growth.

## Limits
Tamil Nadu is not territorially or constitutionally equivalent to Jaffna; caste inequality persists despite social-justice policy.

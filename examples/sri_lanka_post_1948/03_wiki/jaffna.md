# Jaffna

Northern Sri Lankan peninsula and regional centre with long-standing links across the Palk Strait. In the modern corpus it is a recurrent node for missionary education, English-language professional mobility, Tamil politics, caste/temple institutions, civil-war displacement and postwar reconstruction.

## Durable interpretation
Jaffna's historical educational advantage is real but must not be equated with permanent literacy leadership or uniform social equality. Its modern trajectory separates **individual/household human capital** from **territorial capacity to absorb that capital**.

## Limits
Do not treat the peninsula as equivalent to all Sri Lankan Tamils, the whole Northern Province, or Tamil Nadu.

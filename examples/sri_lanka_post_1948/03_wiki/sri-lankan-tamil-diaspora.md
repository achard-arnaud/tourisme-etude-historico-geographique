# Sri Lankan Tamil diaspora

War, displacement and migration moved people, skills, kinship strategies and capital beyond the island. In the causal model the diaspora is neither pure loss nor automatic development finance.

## Durable interpretation
Separate individual/household success abroad from territorial spillovers in Jaffna: remittances, marriage, networks and return investment can preserve capabilities while firms, jobs and tax capacity remain externalized.

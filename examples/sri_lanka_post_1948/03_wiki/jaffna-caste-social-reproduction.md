# Jaffna caste and social reproduction

Caste is retained as a vertical mechanism of status reproduction through temple rights, kinship, marriage, property and networks, while its public political salience changes over time.

## Durable interpretation
Anti-caste law existed in Sri Lanka; Maviddapuram 1968 shows continuing conflict; later ethnonational pressure could subordinate caste as a public cleavage without abolishing private hierarchy.

# Comparator research plan — Jaffna / Tamil Nadu / Indonesia

## Purpose
Use comparison to identify mechanisms, not to rank civilizations or infer monocausal development stories.

## Workstream A — caste and social mobility
**Question:** when does caste remain a private reproduction mechanism, and when does it become an explicit public-policy category?

Compare:
- Jaffna: law against caste discrimination, temple/kudi authority, education, marriage, diaspora;
- Tamil Nadu: non-Brahmin/Dravidian mobilization, reservations, mass education, welfare, professional mobility;
- India-wide only where necessary: Ambedkar/Gandhi, constitutional representation, national reservation framework.

Outcomes to track: higher education, civil service, professions, entrepreneurship, endogamy, temple access, migration.

## Workstream B — language as access architecture
**Question:** how does linguistic decolonization redistribute state access?

Compare:
- Sri Lanka: Sinhala Only, later Tamil official status and English link-language role;
- Indonesia: Malay/Bahasa Indonesia rather than majority Javanese;
- Tamil Nadu/India: regional Tamil identity inside a multilingual federal state with English retained as a major higher-education/economic bridge.

Do not infer growth outcomes from language choice alone.

## Workstream C — conversion of human capital into development
**Question:** why did similar historical strengths in education lead to different territorial outcomes?

Compare starting conditions and confounders:
- market size;
- war/peace;
- federalism/devolution;
- industrial policy;
- infrastructure and urbanization;
- English exposure;
- diaspora/remittances;
- investment climate;
- caste redistribution/social policy.

## Workstream D — social integration after conflict
**Question:** is Sri Lanka moving toward integration, stable segmentation, or renewed territorial separation?

Indicators:
- implementation of official-language rights;
- mixed schools/universities/workplaces;
- internal migration;
- interethnic marriage (if measurable);
- private-sector bilingualism/English;
- common media/culture;
- devolution and local governance;
- diaspora investment and return.

## Evidence gate
A comparator enters the causal spine only after:
1. same mechanism is defined in both cases;
2. time/scale mismatch is documented;
3. major confounders are listed;
4. at least one T0/T1 anchor exists per side;
5. resulting inference changes the Sri Lanka interpretation.

Otherwise keep it as `Petit détour` or backlog.

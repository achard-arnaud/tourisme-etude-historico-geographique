# Run18 — value-capture hypotheses

1. **M1 structural/commodity (U):** downstream blending, branding and retail explain most value capture outside origin.
2. **M2 productive (U):** yield, ageing bushes, replanting and cost disadvantage explain the weak value outcome.
3. **M3 rent allocation (U, priority):** plantation-company capital is rationally redeployed away from agronomy toward higher-return businesses.

## Discriminating test
Compare a decade of RPC/company capex between replanting/mechanisation and hospitality/property/branding/diversification, against reported segment returns and lease horizon.

# A15 — Tsunami 2004 and reconstruction

**Evidence status:** partial

**Related arcs:** `A08_heritage_memory_governance`

**Causal question:** Twenty years on, what changed durably after the tsunami, and did reconstruction aid mitigate or intensify the political conflict in which it landed?

**Period:** 26 December 2004 through the 2005–2006 political consequences, with later reconstruction evaluation used only where dated.

**Primary HIL:** HIL-01 institutions/chronology.

**Secondary HIL:** HIL-02 geography/environment; HIL-04 society/demography; HIL-06 security/coercion.

**Spine:** disaster distribution → reconstruction/buffer policy → contested aid allocation → P-TOMS joint mechanism → judicial/political blockage → bounded assessment of later conflict consequences.

**Drift gate:** The attractive story that the tsunami durably reconciled the parties is tested rather than assumed. Conversely, P-TOMS failure is not treated as a sufficient cause of the return to war.

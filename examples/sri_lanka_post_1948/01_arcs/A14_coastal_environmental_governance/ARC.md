# A14 — Coastal environmental governance

**Evidence status:** partial

**Causal question:** Is Sri Lanka's plastic burden primarily domestic waste mismanagement, imported waste, or maritime accident, and which policy instrument addresses each mechanism?

**Period:** 2017–2023 core slice.

**Primary HIL:** HIL-02 geography/environment.

**Secondary HIL:** HIL-01 institutions/chronology; HIL-03 economy/infrastructure.

**Zoom path:** Z0 beach/nurdles → Z1 western-coast fisheries → Z3 Indian Ocean shipping → Z4 plastics regulation → Z1.

**Spine:** Meethotamulla municipal-waste failure → X-Press Pearl maritime plastic disaster → fishing closure/compensation → single-use plastic regulation → illegal waste-import enforcement.

**Boundaries:** These mechanisms are deliberately not collapsed into one plastic-pollution cause. Accident pollution does not excuse domestic waste governance; domestic waste failure does not explain the 2021 nurdle pulse.

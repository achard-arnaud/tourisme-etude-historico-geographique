# A16 — Tourism shock and fiscal collapse

**Evidence status:** partial

**Related arcs:** `A09_electoral_realignment_2024_2026`

**Causal question:** How much of the 2022 collapse can be attributed to the tourism shock, and how much to fiscal, monetary, agricultural, remittance and debt vulnerabilities?

**Period:** 2019–2022 core slice.

**Primary HIL:** HIL-03 economy/infrastructure.

**Secondary HIL:** HIL-01 institutions/chronology; HIL-04 society/demography; HIL-07 regional/global-system.

**Control case:** Easter 2019 produced a sharp tourism-only shock before the pandemic and before the full crisis configuration. It is used as a sufficiency check, not as a natural experiment with perfect comparability.

**Spine:** Easter tourism shock → 2019 tax reductions → pandemic tourism collapse → remittance/formal-channel stress → fertilizer/import restrictions → pre-existing commercial debt vulnerability → 2022 suspension of external debt service.

**Bounded attribution:** No single cause is allowed to close the default story; every causal bridge must carry a populated `confounders` list and explicit `bounded_by`.

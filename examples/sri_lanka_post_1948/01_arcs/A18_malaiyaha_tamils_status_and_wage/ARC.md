# A18 — Malaiyaha Tamils: status and wage

evidence_status: partial

## Causal question
How did a plantation workforce become a politically weakened/stateless population and later a workforce whose reproduction costs are shared between employer and state?

## TL;DR
This arc separates legal status, wage-setting, housing, union/political brokerage and public subsidy. It explicitly prevents the live 2026 mechanism from being collapsed into one causal statement.

## 2026 gate
Persist wage fact, policy effect and productivity inference as three different records. Government confirms Rs 1,550 minimum daily wage plus Rs 200 attendance incentive and Rs 5,000 million budget provision; no productivity effect follows from that fact alone.

Required separation:
1. `source_fact`: wage level, components, date, company/state split, budget line and covered-workforce denominator.
2. `policy_effect`: effective earnings and coverage consequences; empirical question, including attendance conditions.
3. `inference`: public subsidy changes the return to mechanisation/replanting; causal hypothesis only, bounded by leasehold horizon, terrain, mechanisation/quality constraints, union positions and the fact that underinvestment predates the subsidy by decades.

## Housing baseline
PHDT 2024 reports 116,275 line rooms and about 265,000 plantation-sector families. This establishes persistence of the housing form, not employment-conditioned eviction or slope-risk causality.

## Candidate mechanisms to test
- 1948–49 citizenship legislation → disenfranchisement / deferred political weight.
- Estate union leadership as both labour representation and ethnic-political brokerage → bargaining incentives distinct from a single-purpose union.
- Employment-linked housing and welfare → possible persistence of employer dependence beyond the labour contract.
- Relief and single-access valleys → local labour-market constraints that may differ materially from national labour-market assumptions.

## Drift gate
Claims about citizenship, ethnicity, political brokerage and demographic effects route through historiography/drift audit before promotion.

## Exit rupture / bridge forward
Household-level consequences connect to food, nutrition, housing and migration only after sector-specific official denominators are obtained.

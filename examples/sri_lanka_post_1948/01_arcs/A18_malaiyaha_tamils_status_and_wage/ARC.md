## Causal question
How did a plantation workforce become a politically weakened/stateless population and later a workforce whose reproduction costs are shared between employer and state?

## 2026 gate
Persist wage fact, policy effect and productivity inference as three different records. Government confirms Rs 1,550 minimum daily wage plus Rs 200 attendance incentive and Rs 5,000 million budget provision; no productivity effect follows from that fact alone.

## Housing baseline
PHDT 2024 reports 116,275 line rooms and about 265,000 plantation-sector families. This establishes persistence of the housing form, not employment-conditioned eviction or slope-risk causality.

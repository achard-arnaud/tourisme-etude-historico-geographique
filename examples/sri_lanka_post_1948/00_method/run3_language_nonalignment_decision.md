# Run 3 — anchor vs détour decision

## Promote to trunk
- 1956 as **linguistic decolonization turned majoritarian**: replacement of English is part of the mechanism, not only exclusion of Tamil.
- Jaffna's English/education advantage as a distributive vulnerability after 1956 and during 1970s university standardization.
- 1956–1957 external-sovereignty turn: recovery of British base rights and active neutralism.
- Colombo 1954 → Bandung 1955 → Belgrade 1961 → Colombo NAM summit 1976.
- 1970s economic-decolonization agenda and its overlap, not identity, with inward-looking domestic development policy.

## Keep as side boxes
- Kotelawala–Nehru–Sukarno dispute over what counted as colonialism.
- Nasser/Tito/Sukarno constellation as comparative world context.
- Kadhafi as later radicalization, not founder.
- Malaysian leaders as a correction case, not a main Sri Lankan causal bridge.

## Reject as master causes
- anti-Hindu interpretation of Sinhala Only;
- English retreat as sufficient explanation of current development performance;
- NAM as the cause of import substitution;
- Bandung as formal founding conference of NAM;
- non-alignment as neutrality or Soviet alignment.

# Run 3 — summary

This run integrates a previously underweighted mechanism: **the removal of English as well as the exclusion of Tamil** in the 1956 language turn. It connects that domestic decolonization to Sri Lanka's external-sovereignty strategy and the evolution of non-alignment, while explicitly preventing three overclaims: anti-Hindu intent, English as a master explanation of current underdevelopment, and NAM as the direct cause of inward-looking economics.

# Causal map — post-independence

## Main political-economic trunk
1948 constitutional continuity and citizenship exclusions
→ 1956 linguistic-nationalist rebalancing
→ 1972/1978 constitutional centralisation and executive transformation
→ 1983–2009 civil war and competing sovereignties
→ 2009–2019 postwar infrastructure, centralisation and debt/external reorientation
→ 2019–2022 fiscal/external crisis and mass protest
→ 2022–2026 stabilisation, debt restructuring and electoral realignment.

## Vertical thread — access and social reproduction
colonial education + English + caste/class/property networks
→ differential access to professions and bureaucracy
→ postcolonial redistribution through citizenship/language/district rules
→ anti-caste law + continuing private/religious status reproduction
→ 1968 caste conflict overlaid by defensive Tamil nationalism
→ university-access conflict
→ war disciplines public cleavages while displacing people/institutions
→ marriage/diaspora/kinship become transnational reproduction channels
→ postwar question shifts to whether common gateways are rebuilt.

## Run 5 — conversion of human capital into territorial development
human capital / education
→ access rules and credentials
→ infrastructure + market connectivity
→ firms, public institutions and sectors able to absorb skills
→ jobs / entrepreneurship / suppliers / tax base / services
→ territorial spillovers.

### Jaffna pathway
historical educational specialisation
→ reduced value/access of some inherited gateways after language and university reforms
→ civil war + destruction + maintenance deficit + investment risk
→ displacement/diaspora
→ **capabilities partly preserved transnationally while territorial absorption weakens**
→ post-2009 reconstruction restores inputs but does not automatically recreate firms, networks or integration.

### Tamil Nadu comparator
anti-hierarchy mobilisation + reservations/social policy + expanded education
→ diversification out of caste-bound occupations + infrastructure/welfare
→ larger absorptive Indian market + urban/industrial systems + relative peace
→ stronger territorial conversion of skills.

Gate: this is a **package comparison**, not “reservations cause growth” or “English causes IT”. Tamil Nadu is a federated state, not a direct territorial equivalent of Jaffna.

### Indonesia comparator
Malay-based lingua franca selected instead of majority Javanese
→ common national-language gateway coexisting with regional languages.

Gate: this widens the institutional alternatives available for interpreting Sri Lankan linguistic decolonisation. It does **not** establish Bahasa Indonesia as a cause of Indonesian growth or stability.

## Heritage branch
colonial built environment / archives / collections
→ postcolonial renaming, selective commemoration and heritage institutions
→ tourism/UNESCO property markets + contested urban redevelopment
→ postwar reconstruction of sites such as Jaffna Fort
→ restitution/provenance diplomacy with the Netherlands
→ heritage becomes a live arena of identity, law, property and bilateral politics.

Gate: heritage enters the main trunk only when it changes land/property incentives, reconciliation politics, state legitimacy, international relations or distributional conflict.

## Integration test for 2009–2026
Do not infer integration from one constitution or one infrastructure programme. Test shared gateways:
- effective Sinhala/Tamil/English access;
- mixed universities, firms and workplaces;
- internal mobility and common markets;
- cross-regional infrastructure;
- symmetric public-service access;
- diaspora investment with local spillovers;
- institutions that create repeated intergroup cooperation.

The alternative outcomes are not simply unity/secession: **integrated common gateways**, **stable institutional segmentation**, or **renewed territorial separation** are distinct trajectories requiring separate evidence.

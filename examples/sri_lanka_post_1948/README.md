# Sri Lanka — 1948 à 2026

Worked modern corpus. Run 2 asks what an explicitly colonial/heritage source family can contribute to a post-independence history without allowing heritage to swallow politics, war or political economy.

The answer is a bounded HIL: **memory, heritage, restitution and the reuse of colonial space**. These are not master causes of post-1948 history, but they reveal how successive governments, communities and international institutions negotiate the meaning and economic value of the past.

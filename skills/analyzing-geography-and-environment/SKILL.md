# Analyzing geography and environment

Treat geography as constraint/enabler, not destiny. Identify water systems, relief, seasonal cycles, ecological zones, chokepoints, transport costs and resource distributions. Test whether a geographic factor changes the cost of governing, producing, moving or defending.

# Curating historical map assets

Maps are optional evidence-bearing illustrations, never decorative filler. Search the web only for an arc or `side_story` with `map_eligible=true`. Preserve source URL, publisher/rights, language and local image path. Run a vision review for geographic fit, historical scope, legibility and obvious anachronism; then submit to a human. Only `human_approved` maps may enter a reader.

After approval, record the historical date/precision and a fragment-style caption: what it shows, why it matters here, and limits. Map language must be the document language or English by default. Reader planning selects **at most one approved map per subsection or side-story slot**.

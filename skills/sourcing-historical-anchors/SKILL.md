# Sourcing historical anchors

Use two axes. **Tier** describes how knowledge was produced: T0 primary/material, T1 academic, T2 institutional synthesis, T3 navigation/encyclopedia, T4 field mediation, T5 exploratory. **Anchor role** describes how the source is used in this investigation: canonical anchor, specialist institutional anchor, corroborating bridge, or lead.

A specialist institutional anchor remains T2; co-production by archaeology departments, UNESCO, museums, embassies or foundations does not magically make it T1. Conversely, a narrow T1 article may be excellent evidence for one claim but irrelevant as a general anchor.

Major causal claims should normally have independent corroboration across source roles. Wikipedia can navigate and expose gaps but should not close a contested causal claim alone. Field panels may be promoted to their underlying curated exhibition while the photograph remains T4.

For each source note record tier, anchor role, date, author/institution, scope, claim supported, limitations, provenance, and whether the origin was directly fetched or only discovered through an index/cache.

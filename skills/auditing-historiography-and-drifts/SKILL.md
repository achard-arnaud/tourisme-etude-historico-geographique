# Auditing historiography and drifts

Audit `semantic`, `temporal`, `scale`, `causal`, `source`, `survivorship`, `translation`, and `teleological` drift. Also test vertical bias (elite, monumental, written, state, victor) and horizontal bias (modern borders, fixed ethnicity, centre/periphery, coast/interior, colonial inevitability).

Record the correction next to the claim; do not hide it in an appendix.

# Auditing historiography and drifts

This skill **owns HIL-08_historiography-bias**. Audit semantic, temporal, scale, causal, source, survivorship, translation, teleological, comparator and metric drift plus elite/state/victor and centre/periphery biases.

A correction first updates the affected claim/drift record. If the rejected intuition is useful to a reader, hand it to `composing-side-stories` as `false_lead` or `method`; the side story references the drift evidence and cannot replace it.

Comparator audit checks institutional level, war/peace, market size, transfers, migration, starting conditions and post-treatment variables. Official intent is not observed effect. Record material corrections next to claims and in `07_drifts/`.

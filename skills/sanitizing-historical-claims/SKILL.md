# Sanitizing historical claims

Split each input into `source_fact`, `claim`, `inference`, `tradition`, `analogy`, `question`, or `discarded_lead`. Keep original wording when semantic drift matters. Never let a museum label, chronicle, nationalist narrative, or modern analogy silently become a fact.

## Sanitize checks
- Who is speaking and when?
- Is the wording contemporary or translated?
- What geographic scale is actually supported?
- Is causality asserted or inferred?
- Is a modern category projected backwards?

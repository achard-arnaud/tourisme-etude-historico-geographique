# Analyzing religion, culture and legitimacy

Separate doctrine, institution, ritual and political use. Track how texts, ordination, sacred objects, schools and languages reproduce authority. Shared religion can enable networks without preventing geopolitical conflict. Analogies to modern religious movements are heuristic only and require explicit limits.

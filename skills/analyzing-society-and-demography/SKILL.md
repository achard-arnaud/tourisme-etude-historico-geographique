# Analyzing society and demography

Separate documented migration from assumed population replacement. Track elites, soldiers, merchants, religious specialists, peasants, labourers and diasporas independently. Ask whose mobility changes institutions, production or military capacity. Treat ethnic labels as historically variable unless continuity is demonstrated.

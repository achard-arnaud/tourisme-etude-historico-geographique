# Zooming geographic scales

Use `Z0` object/site, `Z1` local/subregional, `Z2` national/interregional, `Z3` regional, `Z4` global/systemic.

Move between zooms only with an explicit mechanism. A local inscription can evidence a local institution but cannot by itself prove a national pattern. A global trade shift matters only if the local transmission channel is documented. Bridges often connect zooms.

# Zooming geographic scales

Use `Z0` object/site, `Z1` local/subregional, `Z2` national/interregional, `Z3` regional, `Z4` global/systemic.

Move between zooms only with an explicit mechanism. A local inscription can evidence a local institution but cannot by itself prove a national pattern. A global trade shift matters only if the local transmission channel is documented. Bridges often connect zooms.

## Comparison-scale gate
Before comparing two places, label each unit: city, peninsula, district, province, federated state, sovereign state, diaspora or regional network. Do not treat “Tamil Nadu”, “Jaffna” and “Indonesia” as equivalent containers. Normalize population/market size, fiscal/constitutional autonomy, transfers/infrastructure, security jurisdiction, migration/borders and urban-system position.

## Zoom/dezoom narrative
For travel-history synthesis, return from Z3/Z4 comparison to the Z0/Z1 object or place that motivated the question. The dezoom must explain something observable locally rather than becoming detached geopolitical commentary.

When such an excursion is useful for the reader but not itself a causal-trunk step, nominate a `dezoom` side-story candidate. Its later composition record must persist `from`, `to`, `return_to`, the transmission mechanism and the local payoff. Do not mark it promoted here.

## Output
A zoom label (`Z0`–`Z4`) attached to each claim/HIL note plus the explicit mechanism bridging any scale change; optionally a routed `dezoom` candidate for `composing-side-stories` once evidence is stable.

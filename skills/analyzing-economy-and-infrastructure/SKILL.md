# Analyzing economy and infrastructure

Map resource mobilisation: production → extraction/tax → treasury → administration/security → reinvestment. Distinguish physical infrastructure from the institutions required to operate it. For trade, identify commodity, route, intermediary, seasonality, taxation and coercive control. Avoid inventing quantitative crossovers without comparable series.

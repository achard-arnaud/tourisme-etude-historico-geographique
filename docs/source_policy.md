# Source policy

Tier sources by epistemic role rather than brand prestige. T0 primary/material, T1 academic, T2 institutional synthesis, T3 navigation/encyclopedia, T4 field mediation, T5 exploratory. A causal anchor should normally combine independent source roles. Record source scope and limitations. Sanitization precedes tiering: propaganda or a chronicle remains valuable evidence about worldview even when its factual claims require corroboration.

## Intake interpretation boundary

This intake records a field observation and research question, not historical evidence. The observation to test is that Kandy presents little visible monumental urban fortification despite repeated colonial invasion attempts. Competing explanations must remain separable: defence-in-depth through terrain and passes; temporary capture versus durable occupation; local fortifications that are less visually dominant today; deliberate infrastructure underdevelopment; later destruction or transformation of defensive works.

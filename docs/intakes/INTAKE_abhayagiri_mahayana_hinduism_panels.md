## Source semantics

### Panel A — MAHAYANA
Museum synthesis covers Mahayana doctrine; Abhayagiri and Jetavana; Vaitulya/Vetulla traditions; Dhammaruci; later Vajrayana/Tantrayana; named teachers and Bodhisattva sites.

Sanitization:
- Vaitulya/Dhammaruci association with Abhayagiri: promotable when corroborated independently.
- tantric/Vajrayana traces at Abhayagiri: promotable in bounded form with modern scholarship.
- specific transmission by Gunavarman, Vajrabodhi and Amoghavajra: retain as museum lead pending source-specific corroboration.
- Aryadeva as supposed son of a Sri Lankan king: `tradition`, not fact.
- list of colossal Bodhisattva statues: geographical lead until site-by-site cataloguing.

### Panel B — HINDUISM
Museum synthesis covers early Tamil rulers and Brahmanical/Hindu worship; Mahasena-era temples; Pallava/Saiva resurgence; Mahatittha and Gokanna; Tirujnanasambandar / Tiruketisvaram; late first-millennium Hindu bronzes; Chola-period consolidation.

Sanitization:
- do not merge Anuradhapura religious coexistence with the later Kandy Perahera mechanism. Autonomous Saiva/Brahmanical institutions and Buddhist incorporation of devas are different mechanisms.
- `Hinduism found permanent stability as a major religion` is retained as an institutional historiographical formulation, not a measurable fact.
- late first-millennium bronzes must not be conflated with the newly reported 2026 Abhayagiri bronzes in `C-R16-ABH-FIND-001` / `C-R16-ABH-DATE-001`.

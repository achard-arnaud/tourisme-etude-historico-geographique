# Run39 intake — Polonnaruwa monetary debasement

Date: 2026-09-01
Location: Colombo National Museum, Sri Lanka
Input kind: field illustration + analytical hypothesis

## Field observation

Photograph of a museum panel showing a copper coin of Dharmasokadeva dated 1208 CE and a synthesis of Polonnaruwa-period coinage. The panel states that royal coin issue was a monopoly; Vijayabahu I introduced Sinhala-ruler names in Nagari on the existing Lankesvara/Massa type; Parakramabahu I issued copper coins while silver was used to wash base-metal issues; later rulers including Sahasamalla, Lilavati, Codaganga and Dharmasokadeva issued large quantities of copper coin. It also explicitly interprets the equivalence of Kahapana and Masu in literature as indicating depreciation of the currency, while noting continuing circulation of older gold and foreign Chinese, Egyptian and South-Indian coins.

## User hypothesis to test

Could monetary debasement / disappearance of precious-metal coinage be one of the mechanisms contributing to the weakness and end of the Polonnaruwa period?

## Research result

The intuition is useful but must be bounded. Numismatic deterioration is well attested. A specialist SOAS study of the decline of Polonnaruwa, drawing on Codrington, explicitly discusses the disappearance of gold and the turn to copper/base-metal coinage. It considers fiscal drain from Parakramabahu I's military undertakings and foreign garrisons plausible, but stresses two counterpoints: debasement was already visible under Vijayabahu I, and debasement/disappearance of gold cannot by itself prove economic decline because increased transaction volume and trade could also change monetary composition.

Therefore Run39 promotes two separate propositions: (1) the monetary shift/debasement is a historical fact; (2) its role in the collapse is an inference of medium confidence, best treated as a stress indicator/amplifier interacting with fiscal burden, succession instability and invasion rather than a standalone cause.

## Field-photo handling

Binary remains external-only. The photograph is an illustration of the monetary-debasement question and of Dharmasokadeva's copper issue, not independent proof of the causal interpretation.

## Qualified high-value threads

1. **Kusumasana Devi and Vimaladharmasuriya I — dynastic legitimacy through selective appropriation.** Their trajectories connect Portuguese exile/conversion, a Kandyan dynastic claim, Buddhist restoration and the relocation of the Tooth Relic. Keep this as a bounded portrait, not a romance or a pure foreign/native binary.
2. **Kirti Sri Rajasinghe, Saranankara and Upali — Kandy as a transregional Buddhist node.** The 1753 Ayutthaya mission restored higher ordination. This is the strongest dezoom: local religious authority was rebuilt through a maritime and diplomatic Theravada network.
3. **John D'Oyly — knowledge as infrastructure of conquest.** Sinhala language, cultural knowledge, contacts and intelligence helped the British cultivate divisions among Kandyan elites. This supplies the missing mechanism between military difficulty and the political settlement of 1815.
4. **The Ehelepola family — museum memory, not causal proof.** The museum foregrounds martyrdom and bravery. Preserve that framing as evidence of contemporary national memory; do not let it establish contested motives, quoted last words, or a single-cause fall of the kingdom.
